The cast

Three men, one crown.

The cloth gives everyone the same face, so here they are as they might have looked. Nobody painted any of them from life; these are portraits built from what the chroniclers said.

The object

Not a tapestry. Not French. Not finished.

A tapestry is woven on a loom. This is embroidery, wool stitched onto linen with a needle. It was almost certainly made in England, probably at Canterbury, for a Norman bishop, within a generation of the battle. And it stops mid-chase with a ragged edge. The strip above is the museum's own scans, scene by scene, joined end to end; the real thing is nine pieces of linen sewn together, and about 70 metres long.
